About Our Group

Welcome to the McKinney Miracle Al-Anon Family Group website. Al-Anon is a mutual support program for people whose lives have been affected by someone else’s drinking. By sharing common experiences and applying the Twelve Steps, we encourage and support one another.

There is no cost to attend. Our group is self-supporting through voluntary contributions of our members.
